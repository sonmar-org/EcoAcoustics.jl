description: How the user wants Claude to communicate during development sessions
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Keep responses focused on one task at a time. Do not sprawl across multiple tasks or subjects in a single response. Multi-question design within a single task is fine. The problem is covering multiple distinct tasks or subjects simultaneously.

**Why:** Long responses covering multiple unrelated subjects are overwhelming and make it hard to make progress.

**How to apply:** Finish one task completely before moving to the next. Within a task, asking several related design questions at once is acceptable. Do not lose sight of system-level design — keep the architecture in view even while working on a narrow piece.
